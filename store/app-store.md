## Description

Varmint Ridge is a family garden-defense game from JW Manor. Critters come down off the ridge after your tomatoes, corn, and squash. Place scarecrows, lanterns, and snap traps. Keep at least one crop bed alive through twelve named waves, then face the Bandit King.

Wave 1 shows you where to tap. Save and resume on the same device. Settings has sound, music, haptics, color-blind safe, and light or dark for every menu.

The gem shop and Season 1 Pass in this build are mock-only. A grown-up math question opens first. Confirming a pack does not charge a card. There are no ads, no loot boxes, and no random paid rewards.

All ages. East Texas homestead, no people in the art.
