## Full description

Varmint Ridge is a family garden-defense game from JW Manor. Raccoons steal a crop and run. Crows fly the fence. Armadillos dig. Foxes hop. The Bandit King shows up on wave 12.

Place a Scarecrow, Lantern, or Snap Trap, then start the wave. Keep at least one of five crop beds alive. Upgrade defenses, buy Fence, Zeus the farm dog, and Crop Care between waves. Save and resume on the same device.

Wave 1 is a short tutorial. Settings has music, effects, haptics, color-blind safe, and light or dark for every menu.

The gem shop and Season 1 Pass are mock-only. A grown-up must answer a multiplication question first. Confirming a pack does not charge a card. There are no ads, no loot boxes, and no random paid rewards.

All ages. No people in the art.
