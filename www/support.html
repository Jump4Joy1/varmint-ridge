Support

Varmint Ridge is a family garden defense from JW Manor. Write to us at support@backfortyapps.com. The same help page lives at https://backfortyapps.com/varmint-ridge/support/.

How to play

Keep at least one crop bed alive through 12 waves. Place a Scarecrow, Lantern, or Snap Trap in the yard, then tap Start Wave. On a phone, slide your finger and lift to place. Tap a defense to upgrade it or sell it back. Between waves you can buy Fence, Zeus the farm dog, and Crop Care. Wave 1 points at each first step.

Sound, music, haptics, and theme

Title and Pause have Music and Effects sliders. Settings turns Color-blind safe, haptics, and Light / Dark / Auto. The moon/sun chip on the title screen cycles the same theme. Choices save on this device.

Save and resume

Pause, then Save & Quit, stores the current build. Continue on the title screen picks it back up, including coins banked from a mock coin pack.

Grown-up store

Supporter Store asks a multiplication question first. Wrong answers stay on that screen. Cancel does not spend. Packs are fixed: gems, Starter Pack, speed-ups, lessons, skins, and Season 1 with both tracks listed before you buy. This build does not charge a card. There are no ads and no surprise boxes.

Something stuck?

If an older copy of the page is still showing, close the tab and open it again. The service worker uses a new cache name for this build. Widgets still need the signed iOS app on a Mac; they do not install from the website.
