Privacy

Varmint Ridge is a family garden-defense game from JW Manor, published by Back Forty Apps. It is for all ages. It has no ads, no tracking, and no analytics SDKs.

This in-app page is the same policy as https://backfortyapps.com/varmint-ridge/privacy/.

What stays on the device

Scores, initials, the theme you pick, music and effects volume, haptics, color-blind safe, a saved game, gem balance, mock shop records, and cosmetic choices stay in local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a Back Forty Apps server. There is no account and no sign-in.

What we do not collect

We do not collect name, email, location, contacts, photos, advertising IDs, or precise device identifiers. We do not use cookies for advertising. We do not sell or share personal information. Home-screen widgets, when you install the iOS app, read only the best score, initials, and waves opened from the App Group on this phone.

Purchases

The gem shop and Season 1 Pass in this build are mock-only. They sit behind a grown-up math question. Confirming a pack does not charge a card and does not talk to Apple, Google, or any payment processor. Real billing is off. If JW Manor later turns on real in-app purchases, those sales would be handled by Apple or Google, not by this page, and still behind the parental gate.

Children

The game is designed for a family audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A grown-up must answer the math question before the mock store opens.
