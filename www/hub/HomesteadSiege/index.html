How to Play

Three lanes lead to the porch. Tap a lane, or tap the yard, to shine the porch light. Critters close enough head back to the woods.

Raccoons are quick. Crows fly and shy away from a bright lantern. Armadillos are sturdy, and Zeus the dog herds them off the path. Quicker boots make the light ready again sooner.

Ten waves. Wave 10 is the Gate Boar, a big pig at the gate. The porch shop opens between waves. Spend coins you earned on the porch. P pauses. M mutes.

All ages. No ads.
